Title: Shakshuka with Feta and Herbs
Tags: breakfast, vegetarian, gluten-free, middle-eastern
Prep: 10 min  |  Cook: 25 min  |  Serves: 2

## Ingredients
1.5 tbsp olive oil
0.5 large onion, finely diced
0.5 red bell pepper, diced
0.5 yellow bell pepper, diced
2 garlic cloves, minced
0.5 tsp ground cumin
0.5 tsp smoked paprika
0.5/2 tsp ground coriander
0.5/4 tsp cayenne pepper
1 cans (400 g each) crushed tomatoes
0.5 tsp sugar
Salt and black pepper to taste
3 large eggs
50 g feta cheese, crumbled
small bunch of fresh parsley, roughly chopped
Crusty bread to serve

## Method
1. Heat olive oil in a large, deep-sided skillet over medium heat.
2. Add onion and both peppers; cook, stirring occasionally, for 8 minutes until softened.
3. Add garlic, cumin, paprika, coriander, and cayenne. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour in crushed tomatoes and sugar. Season generously with salt and pepper.
5. Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens slightly.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning. The sauce should be rich and slightly spicy.
7. Use a spoon to make 6 wells in the sauce. Crack one egg into each well.
8. Cover the skillet with a lid and cook on medium-low for 6–8 minutes, until whites are set but yolks are still runny.
9. Scatter feta and parsley over the top. Serve directly from the pan with crusty bread.

## Chef Tips
- For a smokier depth, char the peppers over a gas flame before dicing.
- Eggs continue cooking after you remove the pan from heat — pull them a minute early.
- Leftovers reheat well but add a splash of water to the pan and re-cover.
