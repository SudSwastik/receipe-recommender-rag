Title: Miso Ramen with Soft-Boiled Eggs (Variation 11)
Tags: soup, japanese, dinner, noodles
Prep: 30 min  |  Cook: 30 min  |  Serves: 12

## Ingredients
12 large eggs
12 tbsp soy sauce (for marinating eggs)
6 tbsp sesame oil
18 garlic cloves, minced
6 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
12 tbsp white miso paste
6 tbsp soy sauce
3 tbsp mirin
3 litre chicken or dashi stock
1200 ml unsweetened soy milk
1200 g ramen noodles
Toppings: sliced spring onions, nori sheets, bamboo shoots, corn kernels, sesame seeds
Chilli oil to serve

## Method
1. Boil eggs for exactly 6.5 minutes. Transfer to ice water for 10 minutes. Peel and marinate in soy sauce for at least 30 minutes.
2. Heat sesame oil in a large pot. Add garlic and ginger; cook for 2 minutes.
3. Whisk together miso paste, soy sauce, and mirin in a small bowl.
4. Add stock to the pot and bring to a simmer. Stir in miso mixture until dissolved.
5. Add soy milk and gently heat through — do not boil after adding soy milk.
6. Cook ramen noodles separately according to package instructions. Drain and divide among bowls.
7. Ladle hot broth over noodles.
8. Halve the marinated eggs and place on top. Add all toppings.
9. Finish with a drizzle of chilli oil and sesame seeds.

## Chef Tips
- Never boil the broth after adding miso — it destroys the probiotic cultures and flavour.
- Marinate eggs for up to 24 hours for a deeply savoury, jammy result.
- Dashi stock (kombu + bonito flakes) gives authenticity, but good chicken stock works well.
