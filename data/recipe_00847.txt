Title: Classic Beef Burger (Variation 16)
Tags: beef, burger, dinner, american, quick
Prep: 20 min  |  Cook: 15 min  |  Serves: 6

## Ingredients
1050 g ground beef (80/20 fat ratio)
1.5 tsp fine salt
1.5/2 tsp black pepper
1.5/2 tsp garlic powder
6 brioche burger buns
6 slices mature cheddar
Toppings: lettuce, tomato, red onion, pickles
Burger sauce: 4.5 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp ketchup, 1 tbsp yellow mustard, 1 tbsp diced pickles, dash hot sauce

## Method
1. Divide beef into 4 equal portions (about 175 g each). Shape gently into patties slightly wider than the bun (they shrink when cooked).
2. Make a dimple in the centre with your thumb to prevent doming.
3. Season generously on both sides with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Mix burger sauce ingredients. Refrigerate.
5. Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill to very high heat. Brush with a little oil.
6. Cook burgers for 3–4 minutes without pressing or moving. Flip once.
7. Place cheese on the patty and cook for a further 2–3 minutes for medium (slightly pink centre).
8. Toast bun cut sides on the skillet for 1 minute.
9. Assemble: sauce on both bun halves, then lettuce, patty, tomato, onion, and pickles.

## Chef Tips
- Do not overwork the meat when forming patties — it makes them dense and tough.
- The dimple prevents the burger from puffing up into a ball during cooking.
- High heat and no pressing gives the Maillard crust that defines a great burger.
