Title: Classic French Onion Soup (Variation 19)
Tags: soup, french, dinner, comfort-food
Prep: 20 min  |  Cook: 90 min  |  Serves: 8

## Ingredients
2.4 kg yellow onions, thinly sliced
8 tbsp unsalted butter
4 tbsp olive oil
2 tsp sugar
2 tsp fine salt
8 garlic cloves, minced
4 tbsp all-purpose flour
500 ml dry white wine
3 litres good beef stock
4 sprigs fresh marjoram
4 bay leaves
2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
Salt and black pepper to taste
16 thick slices baguette, toasted
400 g Gruyère cheese, grated

## Method
1. Melt butter with olive oil in a large heavy-based pot over medium heat.
2. Add onions, sugar, and salt. Stir to coat. Cook uncovered, stirring every 5–10 minutes, for 50–60 minutes until deep golden-brown and caramelised.
3. Add garlic and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in flour and cook 1 minute.
4. Pour in wine, scraping any browned bits from the base. Boil for 2 minutes.
5. Add stock, thyme, bay leaves, and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 20 minutes.
6. Discard thyme sprigs and bay leaves. Taste and season generously.
7. Preheat grill/broiler to high. Ladle soup into oven-safe bowls and place on a baking tray.
8. Float 2 baguette slices on each bowl and pile with Gruyère.
9. Grill for 3–5 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and golden. Serve immediately.

## Chef Tips
- Patience is everything — the onions must caramelise slowly for full flavour. Rushing makes bitter onions.
- Use homemade beef stock if possible; it makes a profound difference to depth.
- Soup can be made 3 days ahead and refrigerated; only add bread and cheese to order.
