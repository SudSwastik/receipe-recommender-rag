Title: Greek Salad (Horiatiki) (Variation 12)
Tags: salad, vegetarian, greek, gluten-free, summer
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 0 min  |  Serves: 4

## Ingredients
4 large ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
1 large cucumber, halved lengthways and thickly sliced
1 large green bell pepper, cut into rings
1 red onion, thinly sliced
16 Kalamata olives
200 g block feta cheese
2 tsp dried basil
6 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
2 tbsp red wine vinegar
Salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Arrange tomatoes, cucumber, green pepper, and red onion on a large platter.
2. Scatter olives over the vegetables.
3. Place the block of feta on top (do not crumble — authentic Greek salad uses a whole block).
4. Drizzle generously with extra virgin olive oil.
5. Sprinkle dried oregano over the feta and vegetables.
6. Drizzle with red wine vinegar. Season lightly with salt (feta is salty) and generously with black pepper.
7. Allow to sit for 5 minutes before serving so flavours can meld.

## Chef Tips
- Use the best quality olive oil you have — it is the primary flavour vehicle here.
- The salad should not be refrigerator-cold; let tomatoes and cucumber come to room temperature first.
- Real Greek salad contains no lettuce — keep it to the listed vegetables.
