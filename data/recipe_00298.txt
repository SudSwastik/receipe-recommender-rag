Title: Tiramisu (Variation 5)
Tags: dessert, italian, no-bake, coffee, dinner-party
Prep: 40 min  |  Cook: 0 min  |  Serves: 8

## Ingredients
6 large egg yolks
150 g caster sugar
500 g mascarpone, room temperature
300 ml heavy cream
300 ml strong espresso, cooled
4 tbsp Marsala wine or coffee liqueur
200 g savoiardi (ladyfinger) biscuits
Cocoa powder to dust
Dark chocolate shavings to garnish

## Method
1. Whisk egg yolks and sugar in a heatproof bowl over simmering water for 8–10 minutes until pale, thick, and tripled in volume. Remove from heat and cool completely.
2. Fold mascarpone into the cooled egg mixture until smooth and combined.
3. Whip cream to soft peaks. Gently fold into the mascarpone mixture in two additions.
4. Combine cooled espresso and Marsala in a shallow bowl.
5. Quickly dip each savoiardi, one at a time, into the coffee — 2 seconds per side, no more, or they become soggy.
6. Arrange a single layer of dipped savoiardi in a 25 x 30 cm dish.
7. Spread half the mascarpone cream over the savoiardi. Repeat with another soaked savoiardi layer and remaining cream.
8. Dust generously with cocoa powder. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours (overnight is better).
9. Dust with extra cocoa and garnish with chocolate shavings before serving.

## Chef Tips
- Cooking the egg yolks over heat (zabaglione method) makes this safe and gives a more stable cream.
- Dip biscuits briefly — they continue absorbing liquid in the fridge. Over-soaked biscuits create a soggy puddle.
- Tiramisu must be chilled long enough to set; overnight creates the best texture.
