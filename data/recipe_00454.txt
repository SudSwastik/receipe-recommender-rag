Title: Classic Hummus (Variation 8)
Tags: snack, vegan, middle-eastern, gluten-free, dip
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 0 min  |  Serves: 8

## Ingredients
2 cans (400 g each) chickpeas, drained (reserve liquid)
120 ml good tahini
4 tbsp lemon juice (or to taste)
2 garlic cloves, minced
1/2 tsp fine salt
1/4 tsp cumin
4–6 tbsp ice cold water
2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
to serve: olive oil, paprika, fresh basil, whole chickpeas, warm pita

## Method
1. Reserve 4 tbsp of the chickpea liquid (aquafaba). Drain remaining liquid.
2. For the smoothest result: peel the chickpeas by pinching each one to remove the skin.
3. Blend tahini and lemon juice in a food processor for 1 minute until pale and thick.
4. Add garlic and salt; blend 30 seconds.
5. Add chickpeas and cumin. Blend for 2 minutes, pausing to scrape down the sides.
6. With the motor running, drizzle in ice-cold water 1 tbsp at a time until hummus is smooth, light, and pale — this takes 4–5 minutes of processing.
7. Taste and adjust lemon, garlic, and salt.
8. To serve: spread in a bowl with a swirl pattern. Drizzle with olive oil, dust with paprika, scatter with parsley and whole chickpeas.

## Chef Tips
- Blending tahini and lemon first (before adding chickpeas) is the key step for a ultra-creamy result.
- Ice-cold water — not warm — helps the hummus emulsify and turn pale and fluffy.
- The peeling step is optional but makes a significant textural difference.
