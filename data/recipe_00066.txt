Title: Chicken Noodle Soup (Variation 1)
Tags: soup, chicken, comfort-food, dinner, winter
Prep: 20 min  |  Cook: 45 min  |  Serves: 4

## Ingredients
0.7 tbsp olive oil
0.7 large onion, diced
2.7 garlic cloves, minced
2 medium carrots, sliced
2 celery stalks, sliced
1 litres good chicken stock
333.3 g boneless chicken breasts or thighs
1.3 sprigs fresh rosemary
1.3 bay leaves
133.3 g egg noodles or small pasta
Salt and black pepper
fresh basil, chopped, to serve
Juice of 0.7/2 lemon

## Method
1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 6 minutes until softened.
2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Pour in stock, then add whole chicken pieces, thyme, and bay leaves.
4. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 20–25 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
5. Remove chicken and shred into pieces using two forks. Set aside.
6. Discard thyme and bay leaves. Taste the broth and season generously.
7. Add noodles to the simmering broth and cook according to package instructions.
8. Return shredded chicken to the pot. Add lemon juice.
9. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve scattered with parsley.

## Chef Tips
- Use homemade stock for a noticeably superior result.
- Cook noodles directly in the broth only if eating immediately — they absorb liquid and turn mushy on standing.
- Lemon juice at the end is a restaurant trick that brightens the entire soup.
