Title: Granola with Coconut and Pecans (Variation 4)
Tags: breakfast, vegan, meal-prep, gluten-free
Prep: 10 min  |  Cook: 35 min  |  Serves: 2

## Ingredients
0.6 cups rolled oats
0.2 cup pecans, roughly chopped
0.2 cup shredded unsweetened coconut
0.2/2 cup pumpkin seeds
0.2/4 cup sunflower seeds
0.2 tsp ground cinnamon
0.2/2 tsp ground ginger
0.2/4 tsp fine salt
0.2/3 cup coconut oil, melted
0.2/3 cup maple syrup
0.2 tsp vanilla extract
0.2/2 cup dried cranberries or raisins (add after baking)

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 160 °C. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with baking paper.
2. Combine oats, pecans, coconut, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, cinnamon, ginger, and salt in a large bowl.
3. Whisk together coconut oil, maple syrup, and vanilla in a jug.
4. Pour wet ingredients over dry and toss thoroughly until every piece is coated.
5. Spread in an even layer on the prepared baking sheet.
6. Bake for 15 minutes, then rotate the pan and bake for a further 15–20 minutes until deep golden brown.
7. Do not stir during baking — this is what creates the clumps.
8. Remove from oven and allow to cool completely on the tray without disturbing. It will crisp as it cools.
9. Scatter over dried cranberries, then break into clusters. Store in an airtight jar.

## Chef Tips
- Granola looks soft when hot — only judge doneness by colour, not texture.
- Every oven is different; start checking at 25 minutes total to avoid burning.
- Swap pecans for macadamia nuts and cranberries for dried mango for a tropical version.
