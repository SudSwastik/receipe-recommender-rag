Title: Baked Cod with Herbed Breadcrumbs (Variation 8)
Tags: seafood, fish, dinner, healthy
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 20 min  |  Serves: 4

## Ingredients
4 cod fillets (180–200 g each)
Salt and black pepper
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp lemon juice
breadcrumb topping: 80 g panko breadcrumbs, 2 tbsp fresh dill, 1 tbsp fresh marjoram, 2 garlic cloves minced, 30 g parmesan grated, 3 tbsp olive oil, zest of 1 lemon, salt and pepper

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200 °C. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Pat cod dry. Place on tray. Season with salt and pepper. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
3. Combine all breadcrumb topping ingredients in a bowl. Mix until it resembles wet sand.
4. Press the breadcrumb mixture firmly onto the top of each cod fillet.
5. Bake for 15–20 minutes until the crumbs are golden and the fish flakes easily with a fork.
6. The internal temperature should reach 63 °C. Do not overcook.
7. Serve immediately with roasted vegetables or a green salad.

## Chef Tips
- Patting the fish dry ensures the breadcrumbs adhere and don't steam.
- Do not skip the Parmesan — it binds the crumbs and adds savoury depth.
- This works equally well with haddock, halibut, or pollock.
