Title: Roasted Beetroot and Goats Cheese Salad (Variation 3)
Tags: salad, vegetarian, gluten-free, dinner, autumn
Prep: 20 min  |  Cook: 50 min  |  Serves: 2

## Ingredients
300 g mixed beetroot (red and golden), scrubbed
1.5 tbsp olive oil
Salt and black pepper
2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
0.5 tbsp honey
60 g mixed salad leaves
75 g soft goats cheese, crumbled
30 g walnuts, toasted
Small bunch of fresh mint leaves
Dressing: 1.5 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 1 tbsp sherry vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, salt and pepper

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200 °C.
2. Wrap beetroots individually in foil with a drizzle of oil and pinch of salt. Roast for 45–55 minutes until tender when pierced.
3. Allow to cool, then peel using kitchen paper (it slides off easily and saves your hands).
4. Cut beetroots into wedges. Toss in a bowl with balsamic vinegar and honey. Season.
5. Whisk together dressing ingredients.
6. Arrange salad leaves on a platter. Scatter beetroot over, then goats cheese, walnuts, and mint.
7. Drizzle dressing over the salad. Serve immediately.

## Chef Tips
- Roast red and golden beetroots wrapped separately to prevent colour bleeding.
- Cool beetroot completely before adding to leaves or they will wilt immediately.
- Hazelnuts or pecans work equally well in place of walnuts.
