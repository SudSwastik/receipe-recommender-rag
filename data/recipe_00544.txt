Title: Garlic Butter Shrimp Scampi (Variation 10)
Tags: seafood, pasta, italian-american, dinner, quick
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 15 min  |  Serves: 12

## Ingredients
1200 g linguine or spaghetti
1500 g large shrimp, peeled and deveined
15 tbsp unsalted butter
12 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
24 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
3/2 tsp chilli flakes
450 ml dry white wine
Juice of 3 lemon
Zest of 3 lemon
9 tbsp fresh basil, chopped
Salt and black pepper

## Method
1. Cook pasta in well-salted boiling water until 1 minute before al dente. Reserve 200 ml pasta water, then drain.
2. Season shrimp with salt and pepper.
3. Heat 2 tbsp butter and 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer. Cook 1 minute per side until pink. Remove.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining butter and oil. Add garlic and chilli flakes; cook 2 minutes until garlic is golden.
5. Pour in wine. Simmer for 2 minutes until slightly reduced.
6. Add pasta and toss, adding pasta water to loosen into a silky sauce.
7. Return shrimp to the pan. Add lemon juice, lemon zest, and parsley. Toss.
8. Taste and season. Serve immediately.

## Chef Tips
- Shrimp cook extremely fast — remove them the instant they turn pink or they become rubbery.
- Pasta water starch is essential for building the emulsified sauce.
- Use the best white wine you'd actually drink — you'll taste it.
