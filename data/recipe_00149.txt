Title: Guacamole (Variation 2)
Tags: snack, vegan, mexican, gluten-free, dip
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 0 min  |  Serves: 8

## Ingredients
5.3 ripe Hass avocados
Juice of 2.7 limes
1.3/2 small red onion, very finely minced
2.7 garlic cloves, minced
1.3 jalapeño, seeds removed, finely minced
large handful fresh basil, finely chopped
1.3 tsp fine salt
1.3 ripe tomato, seeds removed, finely diced
Tortilla chips to serve

## Method
1. Rinse the minced red onion in cold water for 5 minutes to remove harshness. Drain and dry.
2. Halve, de-stone, and scoop avocado flesh into a bowl.
3. Add lime juice immediately and toss to coat.
4. Mash avocado to your desired texture — chunky mash for rustic, smooth for restaurant-style.
5. Fold in red onion, garlic, jalapeño, and cilantro.
6. Add tomato and stir gently.
7. Taste and season with salt. Add more lime juice if needed.
8. Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole and serve immediately.

## Chef Tips
- Only ripe avocados work — they should yield to gentle pressure and peel away from the skin cleanly.
- Pressing cling film directly on the surface prevents browning if not serving immediately.
- Rinsing the raw onion removes the sharp pungency while keeping its fresh bite.
