Title: Baked Eggs in Tomato Sauce (Variation 15)
Tags: breakfast, vegetarian, gluten-free, italian
Prep: 10 min  |  Cook: 20 min  |  Serves: 10

## Ingredients
10 tbsp olive oil
15 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
5 can (400 g) whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand
5/2 tsp dried thyme
5/4 tsp chilli flakes
Salt and black pepper to taste
20 large eggs
150 g Parmesan, finely grated
fresh oregano leaves to serve
Toasted sourdough to serve

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 200 °C.
2. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and cook 1 minute until golden.
3. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, and chilli flakes. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until sauce has thickened slightly.
5. Taste and adjust seasoning — the sauce should be robust.
6. Make 4 wells in the sauce. Crack one egg into each well.
7. Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake for 8–10 minutes until whites are set but yolks are runny.
8. Scatter Parmesan and fresh basil over the top. Serve from the pan with toasted sourdough.

## Chef Tips
- Add a splash of heavy cream to the sauce just before adding the eggs for a richer result.
- Exact baking time depends on your oven and skillet — start checking at 7 minutes.
- This dish works brilliantly for brunch for a crowd — scale up the sauce in a large baking dish.
