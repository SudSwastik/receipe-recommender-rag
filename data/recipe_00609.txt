Title: Roasted Garlic and Parmesan Broccolini (Variation 11)
Tags: side, vegetarian, gluten-free, quick, healthy
Prep: 5 min  |  Cook: 15 min  |  Serves: 4

## Ingredients
500 g broccolini, trimmed
3 tbsp olive oil
4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
1/2 tsp chilli flakes
Salt and black pepper
40 g Parmesan, finely grated
Juice of 1/2 lemon
Toasted pine nuts (optional)

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 220 °C. Line a large baking tray.
2. Toss broccolini with olive oil, garlic, chilli flakes, salt, and pepper on the tray.
3. Spread in a single layer — do not crowd the pan or it will steam instead of roast.
4. Roast for 12–15 minutes until stems are tender and florets are slightly charred.
5. Transfer to a serving plate. Squeeze lemon juice over and scatter Parmesan.
6. Top with pine nuts if using. Serve immediately.

## Chef Tips
- A single layer on the tray is essential for roasting — use two trays if necessary.
- The charred florets are not burned — they are the best part, adding crunch and bitterness.
- Works equally well with regular broccoli, asparagus, or green beans.
