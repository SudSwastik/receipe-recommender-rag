Title: Vegetable Curry (Sabzi) (Variation 6)
Tags: vegetarian, vegan, indian, gluten-free, dinner
Prep: 20 min  |  Cook: 35 min  |  Serves: 8

## Ingredients
6 tbsp vegetable oil
2 large onion, thinly sliced
8 garlic cloves, minced
4 tsp fresh ginger, grated
4 tsp ground cumin
3 tsp ground coriander
2 tsp turmeric
2 tsp garam masala
2/2 tsp chilli powder
4 medium potatoes, cubed
2 cup cauliflower florets
2 can (400 g) diced tomatoes
2 cup frozen peas
800 ml coconut milk
Salt to taste
fresh cilantro, naan, and rice to serve

## Method
1. Heat oil over medium heat. Cook onion for 10 minutes until deeply golden.
2. Add garlic and ginger; cook 2 minutes.
3. Add all dry spices and cook 1 minute, stirring, until fragrant.
4. Add potatoes and cauliflower; stir to coat in spices.
5. Add diced tomatoes and coconut milk. Season with salt. Stir well.
6. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer covered for 20 minutes until potatoes are tender.
7. Stir in peas and cook a further 5 minutes.
8. Taste and adjust seasoning and spice level.
9. Serve with steamed rice or naan and fresh cilantro.

## Chef Tips
- Allowing onions to deeply brown builds the flavour base — light golden is not enough.
- Cut potatoes and cauliflower into similar sizes for even cooking.
- Add a can of chickpeas for extra protein and texture.
