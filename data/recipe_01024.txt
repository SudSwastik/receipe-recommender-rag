Title: Bircher Muesli with Apple and Walnuts (Variation 20)
Tags: breakfast, vegetarian, meal-prep, healthy, no-cook
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 0 min  |  Serves: 3

## Ingredients
1.5 cups rolled oats
0.8 cup whole milk or oat milk
0.8 cup plain Greek yoghurt, plus extra to serve
1.5 tbsp honey or maple syrup
0.8 tsp vanilla extract
0.8/2 tsp ground cinnamon
1.5 apples, unpeeled and coarsely grated
Juice of 0.8/2 lemon
0.8/2 cup walnuts, roughly chopped
0.8/4 cup raisins or sultanas
Fresh berries and extra honey to serve

## Method
1. Combine oats, milk, yoghurt, honey, vanilla, and cinnamon in a large bowl. Stir well.
2. Grate the apples directly into the bowl to catch all the juice. Squeeze over lemon juice immediately to prevent browning.
3. Stir in walnuts and raisins.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
5. In the morning, stir the muesli. Add a splash more milk if you prefer a looser consistency.
6. Spoon into bowls and top with a dollop of Greek yoghurt, fresh berries, and a drizzle of honey.

## Chef Tips
- Bircher improves with age — make a big batch on Sunday and it keeps for up to 4 days.
- Swap walnuts for pecans, almonds, or a seed mix to vary the texture.
- Steel-cut oats can be used for a chewier texture but need 8+ hours soaking.
