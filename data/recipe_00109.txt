Title: Green Smoothie Bowl (Variation 2)
Tags: breakfast, vegan, gluten-free, no-cook, healthy
Prep: 10 min  |  Cook: 0 min  |  Serves: 12

## Ingredients
12 frozen bananas, sliced
6 cup frozen mango chunks
12 cups baby spinach
6/2 cup coconut milk
6 tbsp almond butter
6 tsp spirulina powder (optional)
Toppings: sliced fresh fruit, granola, chia seeds, hemp hearts, shredded coconut

## Method
1. Combine frozen bananas, frozen mango, spinach, coconut milk, almond butter, and spirulina in a high-powered blender.
2. Blend on high, starting on low speed and gradually increasing, until completely smooth.
3. The mixture should be thick — if the blender struggles, add coconut milk 1 tbsp at a time.
4. Pour into bowls. It should be thick enough that toppings sit on top without sinking.
5. Arrange toppings decoratively: sliced fruit, granola, chia seeds, hemp hearts, and coconut.
6. Serve immediately.

## Chef Tips
- Freeze bananas in advance in a ziplock bag — they're essential for creaminess and chill.
- The bowl should be eaten immediately before it melts and becomes a smoothie.
- Add a scoop of vanilla protein powder to make it more filling as a post-workout meal.
