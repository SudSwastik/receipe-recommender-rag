Project 1 — Recipe Recommender RAG
Scenario
A food startup wants a chatbot that answers user questions from a library of 10,000 recipe PDFs. Users ask things like "What can I make with leftover chicken and lemon?" or "Give me a low-carb dessert under 30 minutes." Your job is to build the ingestion pipeline and a working retrieval loop — then diagnose why it sometimes returns irrelevant recipes.


Part A — Ingestion Pipeline
Task 1 · Chunk the Recipes
You are given a folder of recipe text files. Each file has the structure below. Design a chunking strategy that treats each section as its own chunk — ingredients, method, and tips should never be merged into one embedding.

Q1  Chunk design
Describe your chunking strategy in plain English. What is the unit of a chunk — one section, one recipe, one instruction step? Justify the choice given that queries will mix ingredient constraints with dietary filters.
Hint: Think about what information a single embedding needs to carry for a cosine search to work.
Q2  Metadata schema
Define the metadata fields you will attach to each chunk (e.g., recipe_id, section, tags, prep_time_mins). Write a Python dataclass or dict that represents one chunk record.
Q3  Overlap decision
The method section of some recipes spans 800 words. Would you add chunk overlap here? How many characters, and why?
