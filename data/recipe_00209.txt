Title: Classic French Omelette (Variation 4)
Tags: breakfast, quick, gluten-free, french, low-carb
Prep: 5 min  |  Cook: 5 min  |  Serves: 4

## Ingredients
12 large eggs
4 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cut into small cubes
4 tsp water
Pinch of fine salt
White pepper to taste
8 tbsp finely chopped parsley or herbs of choice
120 g Gruyère or cheddar, finely grated (optional)

## Method
1. Crack eggs into a bowl, add water, salt, and white pepper. Beat vigorously with a fork until fully combined and slightly frothy.
2. Place a 20 cm non-stick skillet over high heat. Add butter.
3. As soon as the butter stops foaming, pour in the eggs.
4. Immediately stir briskly with a rubber spatula in small circles, while simultaneously shaking the pan back and forth.
5. After about 20 seconds the eggs should be mostly set but still slightly wet on top. Stop stirring.
6. If using cheese, scatter it over the centre now.
7. Tilt the pan at 45 degrees. Use the spatula to fold the omelette away from you, rolling it toward the far edge of the pan.
8. Tip the omelette onto a warm plate, seam-side down. It should be pale yellow with no browning.
9. Scatter chives over the top. Serve immediately.

## Chef Tips
- The entire process takes under 90 seconds — have your plate ready before you start.
- A pale, unbrowned exterior is the hallmark of a proper French omelette; lower heat if you see colour.
- Season only the eggs, not the pan — salt draws moisture that can cause sticking.
