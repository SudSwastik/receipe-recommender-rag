Title: Vegetable Fried Rice (Variation 19)
Tags: rice, vegetarian, asian, quick, meal-prep
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 15 min  |  Serves: 4

## Ingredients
4 cups cooked jasmine rice (day-old, refrigerated)
3 tbsp sesame oil
1 tbsp vegetable oil
4 garlic cloves, minced
1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
3 spring onions, sliced (white and green parts separated)
1 cup frozen peas and carrots, thawed
1 red bell pepper, finely diced
3 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp oyster sauce (or extra soy for vegan)
1 tsp sesame oil (to finish)
3 eggs, beaten
Salt and white pepper
Chilli oil and extra soy sauce to serve

## Method
1. Ensure rice is cold and not clumped — break up any lumps with your hands.
2. Heat a wok over very high heat. Add oils.
3. Add garlic, ginger, and white spring onion parts. Stir-fry for 30 seconds.
4. Add bell pepper and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
5. Add cold rice and spread across the wok. Leave undisturbed for 1 minute to develop a crust, then toss.
6. Push rice to the side. Add eggs to the empty space and scramble until just set. Fold into rice.
7. Add peas and carrots. Drizzle over soy sauce and oyster sauce. Toss to combine.
8. Stir in green spring onion tops and a finishing drizzle of sesame oil. Taste and season.
9. Serve immediately with chilli oil and soy sauce on the side.

## Chef Tips
- Day-old refrigerated rice is mandatory — fresh rice steams and clumps rather than frying.
- Maximum heat prevents sticking and creates the characteristic 'wok hei' smoky flavour.
- Add any leftover vegetables or proteins — fried rice is ideal for cleaning out the fridge.
