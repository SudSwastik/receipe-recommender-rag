Title: Classic Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes (Variation 11)
Tags: breakfast, vegetarian, quick, american
Prep: 10 min  |  Cook: 20 min  |  Serves: 12

## Ingredients
6 cups all-purpose flour
6 tbsp granulated sugar
6 tsp baking powder
3/2 tsp baking soda
3/2 tsp fine salt
6 cups buttermilk, room temperature
6 large eggs
9 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and cooled
3 tsp pure vanilla extract
Neutral oil or butter for the pan
Maple syrup and fresh berries to serve

## Method
1. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a separate jug, whisk buttermilk, eggs, melted butter, and vanilla until combined.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and fold gently until just combined — lumps are fine; do not overmix.
4. Rest the batter for 5 minutes while you heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat.
5. Brush the pan lightly with oil or butter.
6. Pour 1/3 cup batter per pancake onto the skillet. Cook until bubbles form across the surface and the edges look set, about 2–3 minutes.
7. Flip and cook for a further 1–2 minutes until golden and cooked through.
8. Keep finished pancakes warm in a 90 °C oven on a wire rack while you finish the batch.
9. Serve stacked with maple syrup and fresh berries.

## Chef Tips
- Room-temperature buttermilk hydrates the flour more evenly and gives a lighter crumb.
- The baking soda reacts with the buttermilk acid — don't mix and let batter sit more than 15 minutes or it will lose lift.
- For extra-fluffy pancakes, separate the eggs, whisk the whites to soft peaks, and fold in last.
