Title: Avocado Toast with Poached Eggs
Tags: breakfast, vegetarian, quick, healthy
Prep: 10 min  |  Cook: 10 min  |  Serves: 8

## Ingredients
16 thick slices sourdough bread
8 ripe avocados
Juice of 4 lemon
4/4 tsp garlic powder
Flaky sea salt and black pepper
4/2 tsp chilli flakes
16 large eggs
8 tbsp white vinegar
Handful of rocket (arugula)
Extra virgin olive oil to drizzle
Microgreens or sprouts to garnish (optional)

## Method
1. Bring a wide saucepan of water to a gentle simmer. Add white vinegar.
2. Toast sourdough slices until golden and crisp.
3. Halve, de-stone, and scoop avocado flesh into a bowl. Add lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Mash to a chunky paste.
4. Spread avocado generously over each toast slice.
5. Crack each egg into a small cup. Create a gentle whirlpool in the simmering water and slide eggs in one at a time.
6. Poach for 3 minutes for a runny yolk. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper.
7. Place a small handful of rocket on each toast, then top with a poached egg.
8. Season with flaky salt, black pepper, and chilli flakes. Drizzle with olive oil.

## Chef Tips
- Very fresh eggs hold together best when poaching — look for eggs less than 1 week old.
- Add a teaspoon of nutritional yeast to the avocado mash for a subtle cheesy depth.
- Prepare a large batch of poached eggs in advance and store in cold water in the fridge for up to 2 days; reheat in hot water for 1 minute.
