Title: Warm Quinoa Salad with Roasted Vegetables (Variation 5)
Tags: salad, vegan, gluten-free, healthy, meal-prep
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 30 min  |  Serves: 6

## Ingredients
2.2 cups quinoa, rinsed
4.5 cups vegetable stock
1.5 red bell pepper, diced
1.5 yellow bell pepper, diced
1.5 zucchini, diced
1.5 small eggplant, diced
1.5 red onion, cut into wedges
4.5 tbsp olive oil
1.5 tsp smoked paprika
Salt and black pepper
90 g baby spinach
45 g toasted pine nuts
fresh cilantro and mint, chopped
Dressing: 4.5 tbsp lemon juice, 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, salt, pepper

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 220 °C.
2. Toss all diced vegetables and onion in olive oil, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread on 2 large baking trays.
3. Roast for 25–30 minutes, turning once, until golden and slightly charred at the edges.
4. Meanwhile, combine quinoa and stock in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
5. Whisk together dressing ingredients.
6. In a large bowl, combine warm quinoa, roasted vegetables, and baby spinach. Toss with dressing.
7. Transfer to a platter. Top with pine nuts and fresh herbs.

## Chef Tips
- Rinsing quinoa removes the bitter saponin coating — never skip this step.
- The salad is equally good warm or at room temperature, making it excellent for meal prep.
- Add crumbled feta or roasted chickpeas for extra protein.
