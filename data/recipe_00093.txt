Title: New York Cheesecake (Variation 1)
Tags: dessert, cheesecake, american, baking
Prep: 30 min  |  Cook: 90 min  |  Serves: 10

## Ingredients
Base: 166.7 g digestive biscuits, 80 g butter melted, 2 tbsp sugar
Filling: 750 g full-fat cream cheese, room temp; 250 g sour cream; 200 g caster sugar; 4 large eggs, room temp; 2 tsp vanilla extract; 1 tbsp lemon juice; 2 tbsp all-purpose flour

## Method
1. Preheat oven to 160 °C. Grease a 23 cm springform tin. Wrap base and sides tightly in two layers of foil.
2. Crush biscuits to fine crumbs. Mix with melted butter and sugar. Press firmly into the tin base. Refrigerate.
3. Beat cream cheese with an electric mixer on medium until smooth. Add sugar and beat until combined.
4. Add flour, then eggs one at a time, mixing on low after each addition.
5. Mix in sour cream, vanilla, and lemon juice on low speed until just combined. Do not overmix.
6. Pour filling over chilled base. Tap tin gently on the counter to release air bubbles.
7. Place tin in a large roasting dish and pour boiling water in to come 2 cm up the side of the tin.
8. Bake for 80–90 minutes. The edge should be set but the centre should still have a wobble.
9. Turn oven off. Leave cheesecake in the oven with the door ajar for 1 hour.
10. Remove, cool to room temperature, then refrigerate overnight before serving.

## Chef Tips
- All dairy ingredients must be at room temperature — cold cream cheese creates lumps.
- The water bath creates steam, preventing cracks and creating an even, silky texture.
- Cheesecake improves after 24 hours in the fridge — make it the day before serving.
