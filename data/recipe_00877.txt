Title: Thai Coconut Chicken Soup (Tom Kha Gai) (Variation 17)
Tags: soup, thai, gluten-free, dinner
Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 25 min  |  Serves: 3

## Ingredients
1.5 cans (400 ml each) full-fat coconut milk
375 ml chicken stock
3 stalks lemongrass, bruised and cut into 5 cm pieces
4.5 slices galangal or fresh ginger (3 mm thick)
3 kaffir lime leaves, torn
2.2 Thai bird's eye chillies, bruised
375 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
150 g oyster mushrooms, torn
2.2 tbsp fish sauce
0.8 tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar
Juice of 1.5 limes
fresh dill to serve
Thinly sliced red chilli to serve

## Method
1. Combine coconut milk and chicken stock in a large saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a gentle simmer.
2. Add lemongrass, galangal, lime leaves, and chillies. Simmer for 5 minutes to infuse.
3. Add sliced chicken and mushrooms. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
4. Season with fish sauce and sugar. Stir to dissolve.
5. Remove from heat and add lime juice. Taste — the soup should be salty, sour, and slightly sweet.
6. Adjust with more fish sauce (salt), lime juice (sour), or sugar (sweet) to balance.
7. Ladle into bowls, discarding lemongrass and galangal if preferred.
8. Top with fresh cilantro and sliced chilli.

## Chef Tips
- Galangal is traditional and distinct from ginger — use it if you can find it at Asian grocers.
- Do not boil after adding lime juice or the soup will become bitter.
- Kaffir lime leaves are key; freeze extras for future use.
